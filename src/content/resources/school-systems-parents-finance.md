Schools feel integration failure immediately: a child is enrolled in academics but not in billing; a parent is messaged from a list that finance does not recognise.

## One student record

Admissions, class placement, invoicing and the parent portal should share an identity. That sounds obvious. It is rarely how the tools were bought.

## Term-time is the test

Peak load is not a theoretical concern. Fee runs, report cards and enrolment windows coincide. Systems have to be proven against that calendar, not a quiet week in the lab.

## What we typically deliver

A school ERP covering enrolment, academics, fees and family communication — with training for administrators and a support path through the first full term.
