Open source is not a strategy by itself. It is a licensing and extension model. The organisation still needs a system design, a data model, integrations, and people who can operate the result.

## What you gain

You can inspect the code, extend modules, and avoid a purely proprietary ceiling on cost. For many growing organisations in the region, that combination is decisive.

## What you still fund

Hosting, backups, upgrades, custom modules, bank and tax integrations, and training. A cheap licence with an unbounded customisation list is not cheap.

## Our stance

We recommend Odoo when it is the right fit — and we are explicit when a thinner custom application would be cleaner. The goal is software the organisation can live with, not a platform for its own sake.
