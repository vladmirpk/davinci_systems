ERP programmes slow down when the organisation and the software are asked to change at the same time, with no one named as owner of either.

## Start with the work

Map how invoices, stock, approvals and reporting happen today. The first release should cover the path that already keeps the organisation alive — not a catalogue of every module in the brochure.

## Bound the first release

A useful first go-live is smaller than the five-year vision. Accounting plus sales, or inventory plus purchasing, beats a simultaneous cutover of everything the vendor can demo.

## Train the people who will run it

Configuration without training produces a system that exists and a team that still lives in spreadsheets. Role-based sessions, written job aids and a named internal champion are part of delivery, not extras.

Davinci Systems treats go-live as a supported transition: hypercare, issue triage, and a backlog for the next increment.
